Hi, I'm Chenxi Ma, a third-year undergraduate student currently interning at UW Systems Lab under the guidance of Baris Kasikci.

My interests lie in LLM inference optimization and GPU systems. I explore how model capabilities connect with the systems that execute them.

My technical interests include inference runtimes, GPU scheduling, mixture-of-experts models, and communication and computation in distributed inference. I'm also interested in co-design across model architectures, agent harnesses, hardware, and infrastructure.

I'm a contributor to vLLM, working on MoE expert parallelism, dual-batch overlap (DBO), and NIXL-based dispatch in Model Runner V2 (MRV2).

This page brings together my projects and research interests. My personal blog has just launched, where I'll share technical articles and experiment notes.

Selected Projects

mini-DeepInfer

An inference runtime, from request to next token.

A readable re-implementation of DeepInfer's core design, running the real DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash on 4 × B200. Every optimization is recorded in a GitHub issue with its baseline, profile, correctness tests and measured result. Prefill throughput is up about 4× so far, and still 8.4× behind the official engine.

MoE expert parallelism for vLLM.
